How the warnings are made

River floods

Every day the 51-member GloFAS v4 ensemble forecasts river discharge 30 days ahead on a ~5 km river network. Each site sits on the modelled river nearest the town that carries a real share of the local flow.

Thresholds are the 2-, 5- and 20-year floods of the same model's own daily discharge (), from a Gumbel fit (L-moments) to water-year (Oct–Sep) maxima. Comparing a model with its own climate removes its bias, which is how GloFAS itself issues flood signals — so flows are shown as multiples of the 2-year flood.

Flash-flood rain

Small, steep catchments flood faster than a 5 km river model can see, so cities with a flash-flood history are also tracked through rainfall: the 51-member ECMWF IFS ensemble (0.25°), summed over Iran local days and over 3-day windows, against the 2-, 5- and 20-year 1-day and 3-day rainfall of ERA5 (0.25°, ) computed the same way.

Rain alerts count days 1–; later days are shown faded. The page updates within about an hour of each new model run: GloFAS once a day, the ECMWF ensemble every 6 hours.

Levels

Below large dams the real flow depends on release decisions; those sites are marked. Not an official warning — always follow the Iran Meteorological Organization and local crisis-management authorities.
